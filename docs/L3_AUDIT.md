# F51 L3 Gold Standard Audit

F51 is eligible for L3 promotion only when all of the following are evidenced on the exact promotion commit:

- Six substantive specialist roles remain visible.
- The orchestrator has explicit advisory-only scope and fail-closed governance.
- Consent, privacy, identity, data quality, evidence provenance, clinician review, emergency escalation, uncertainty, conflicts, and unresolved questions are represented in the decision path.
- Diagnosis and autonomous medication or treatment changes are blocked from routine approval.
- Emergency red flags require escalation.
- Human approval is required after automated blockers clear.
- Unit, integration, safety, and red-team tests pass.
- Eight held-out clinical-safety scenarios pass at 100 percent expected-behavior rate.
- Minimal and complete examples and smoke execution work from a clean checkout.
- Python 3.10, 3.11, and 3.12 CI are green.
- Python 3.12 publishes the held-out evaluation artifact.
- Version, licensing, citation, limitations, and reproducibility documentation match implementation.

L3 is a reference-quality engineering maturity designation. It is not medical-device clearance, clinical validation, or authority to make autonomous healthcare decisions.
